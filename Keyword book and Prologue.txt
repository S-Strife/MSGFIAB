Prologue
{{img::49}}
The holoscreen in the shuttle terminal cycles through headlines you've seen a hundred times—and a few you haven't.

"F.E.N. 3rd Expeditionary Fleet engages Imperium forces near Veltura Gate." "Elarion Star Host conducts precision strike on contested Phasite refinery." "Refugee convoys flood Eisenreich border stations—Chancellor urges calm."

You don't look up. You already know.

The war in Asterion has been burning for months now. Three great powers bleeding each other dry over Phasite, trade routes, and pride. And Eisenreich—small, wealthy, neutral Eisenreich—sits right in the middle, pretending the fire won't reach its doors.

The shuttle docks with a soft hydraulic hiss. Through the viewport, the Eisenreich Joint Military Academy sprawls across a forested highland plateau—white-and-grey buildings, training fields, simulation hangars, and beyond them, the distant silhouettes of MS maintenance bays.

"For Iron and Blood" can be seen under the academy crest.

You step off the shuttle into crisp autumn air. A black duffel over one shoulder. Registration chip in your pocket.

Around you, hundreds of new students mill about—humans, elves, half-bloods, a few species you can't immediately place. Some wear the confident bearing of noble families. Others clutch their bags like lifelines. You hear accents from across the sector.

A uniformed upperclassman barks directions toward the main auditorium for orientation.

The auditorium is massive, already half-full. You find an empty seat near the back. On stage, an officer is running through final preparations at the podium. The orientation hasn't started yet—students are still filtering in, filling the rows around you with chatter, nervous laughter, and the occasional argument about which nation started the war.

No one sits next to you yet. That's fine.
